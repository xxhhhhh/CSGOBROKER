Review of XCase

XCase is a newly launched platform for CS2 case openings and skin upgrades. The service offers more than 20 cases across different price categories, ranging from budget-friendly options to expensive collections featuring rare items. Its primary focus is on traditional case-opening mechanics, complemented by a feature for upgrading acquired skins.

Users can open up to five cases simultaneously or use the Upgrade mode to exchange lower-value items for more expensive skins with a defined probability of success. The upgrader provides preset success probabilities and multipliers of x2, x4, and x8. The website features a modern interface with straightforward navigation and convenient access to its main gaming functions.

Deposits are available through bank cards using the SBP instant payment system, as well as cryptocurrencies including USDT (TRC-20), BTC, ETH, BNB, and TRON. CS2 skin deposits are also supported through the third-party service Skinsback. Acquired items are stored in the user's internal inventory and can be withdrawn to their Steam account. Transaction fees and conditions depend on the selected payment method.

Overall, XCase is a promising platform for users interested in CS2 case openings and skin upgrades. Its varied case prices, multiple-case opening feature, and flexible upgrade settings provide a functional gaming experience. Nevertheless, the project is relatively new, meaning its reputation is still developing, which users should consider before using the platform.

Detailed Rating

Trust

XCase is a relatively new CS2 case-opening platform launched in 2026. The website footer lists the legal details of LLC "Transtrade", a company registered in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan. Providing information about the operator improves the platform's transparency, but its short operating history and near-total lack of independent reviews make it difficult to objectively assess its reliability. Another drawback is the absence of a publicly verified Provably Fair system that would allow users to independently verify the results of gaming rounds.

Support

XCase's customer support currently offers fewer contact options than larger platforms. The website lacks a dedicated live chat for direct communication with a support agent, limiting users' ability to quickly resolve issues related to deposits, gaming activities, and item withdrawals. Information about support hours and average response times is also limited. However, given the platform's short operating history, there is not yet enough data to objectively evaluate the quality of its customer service.

Payments

XCase supports several deposit methods, including bank cards through the Russian Faster Payments System (SBP), cryptocurrencies such as USDT (TRC-20), BTC, ETH, BNB, and TRON, as well as CS2 skins through the third-party service Skinsback. Obtained items can be withdrawn through the Steam trading system. The variety of payment methods provides convenience for users with different preferences, although exact fees, minimum amounts, and transaction processing times are not disclosed in sufficient detail. The availability of direct cash withdrawals to bank cards or cryptocurrency wallets has not been confirmed.

Playability

XCase primarily focuses on two game modes: case opening and Upgrade. The platform offers more than 20 themed cases across various price categories, with the option to open up to five cases simultaneously. Upgrade mode provides success probability settings of 40%, 65%, and 90%, along with x2, x4, and x8 multipliers for choosing the balance between risk and potential rewards. Despite the absence of contracts, case battles, and other additional features, the available modes provide a reasonably varied gaming experience.
