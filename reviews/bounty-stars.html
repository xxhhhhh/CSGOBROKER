The platform uses a Provably Fair system based on the SHA-2 algorithm, allowing players to verify each game outcome. However, the lack of licensing, independent audits, and anonymous ownership reduces overall transparency. Despite this, the platform is actively developing, features SSL encryption, and regularly updates its functionality following its rebranding, maintaining positive feedback for its visuals and animations.

Users note the team's responsiveness and the availability of a Russian-speaking chat. However, in non-standard cases (e.g., large withdrawals), templated responses and processing delays may occur. Overall, the support team shows stability and reasonable engagement.

Payments

While transactions are processed quickly (within 60 minutes), they often come with notable fees, especially during item conversion. Withdrawals are only available via marketplaces like SkinPay, which can lower the effective value of winnings. Fiat methods are not supported, and limits depend on account activity.

Playability
