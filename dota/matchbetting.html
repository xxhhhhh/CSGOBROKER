Item gambling involves the use of virtual items, typically cosmetic in-game items, as a form of virtual currency to bet on the outcome of professional matches or other games of chance. This practice is commonly associated with the community of the popular game Dota 2, where players can obtain decorative weapon covers known as "skins" through purchase or in-game rewards. These skins can hold value, with rarer skins commanding higher prices. Skin gambling websites provide a platform for players to utilize their skins as bets on esports matches, allowing them to wager as many skins as they wish. During the betting process, the skins being wagered are removed from the players' Steam library and locked. In the event of a win, players receive their own skins back, as well as the skins that their opponent bet and lost, which are returned to their Steam library.
