En

Plinko - A popular gambling game mode that has gained widespread popularity on CS2 and CS:GO skin gambling sites. In this mode, players place bets with skins and drop chips from the top of a vertical board filled with obstacles and slots. The goal of the game is for the chip to fall down, bouncing off obstacles, and land in one of the bottom slots, each of which has its own win multiplier. The higher the multiplier, the greater the player's winnings. The risk and potential winnings depend on the board's structure and the placement of the high-multiplier slots. Various sites offer their own versions of the Plinko mode with different features and unique elements, providing players with many opportunities to win.
