Rust Jackpot is a form of gambling within the Rust game, where players place bets using skins or credits on a Jackpot Game. The game follows a traditional jackpot format, where players select their bet amount and contribute it to the pot. Once all bets are placed, the Random Number Generator (RNG) determines the winner. The higher the bet value, the higher the chances of winning. Rust Jackpot sites typically apply a commission to the jackpot, which can range from 0 to 10 percent. Following Valve's introduction of a 7-day trade hold on traded items, the popularity of skin Jackpot sites declined significantly, resulting in the closure of many such platforms.
