Sign-Up Bonuses: Common Types and What to Check Before Creating an Account

A sign-up bonus is a welcome reward offered to a new user after account creation and completion of the stated eligibility steps. It can be a free case, internal balance, promo code, discount, free attempt in a game mode, or an additional percentage on the first deposit.

Registration itself does not always trigger the reward automatically. Some sites require email or phone verification, while others require a promo code, a linked Steam account, a first deposit, or a small activity requirement. The useful comparison is therefore the complete path to receiving the reward, not only its advertised size.

CSGOBROKER evaluates sign-up bonuses separately from deposit bonuses, daily rewards, and giveaways. This makes it easier to distinguish a genuinely free welcome reward from an offer that requires additional spending.

Common Types of Sign-Up Bonuses

Free case

A new user receives one or more openings of a special case. Check the complete item pool, outcome probabilities, and whether the result can be withdrawn without making an additional deposit.

Fixed bonus balance

The service credits a stated amount of internal currency after registration. It may only be usable in selected modes or require conditions before withdrawal.

Promo-code welcome reward

A code must be entered during registration or from the account profile. The same site can have several codes with different rewards, so the active terms should be checked.

Discount or free attempt

Instead of balance, a platform may provide a case discount, free spin, upgrade attempt, or another one-time function. Its value depends on the normal mode price and result rules.

First-deposit bonus

This is sometimes marketed together with the welcome reward but requires funding the account. It should also be compared under deposit bonuses.

Bundle of welcome rewards

Some services combine a free case, promo code, small balance, and later deposit bonus. Each component should be evaluated separately because use and withdrawal terms can differ.

What to Check Before Signing Up

A welcome bonus is useful only when the requirements are clear before account creation rather than appearing afterward.

Whether a deposit is required

Confirm whether the reward is truly registration-only or requires a first deposit. An advertisement using “up to” can combine a free component with a deposit-related one.

Whether a promo code is required

Check when the code must be entered: before account creation, immediately after registration, or before the first deposit. A missed step may not be recoverable.

Required verification steps

Email, phone, Steam linking, or identity verification can be part of eligibility. It is better to know this before registering if you prefer not to provide additional information.

Whether the reward is withdrawable

Free balance or an item can have a minimum withdrawal, wagering requirement, mode restriction, or a requirement to fund the account first.

Expiry

Some welcome rewards must be activated or used within hours or days. An unused bonus can disappear after the deadline.

Regional availability

The welcome offer, promo code, or withdrawal method can be unavailable in some countries even when the website itself remains accessible.

How CSGOBROKER Rates Sign-Up Bonuses

We compare the real value for a new user rather than the largest promotional number or steps that were not obvious before registration.

Genuinely free component

We separate rewards credited for registration itself from deposit bonuses and other benefits that require spending.

Ease of claiming

We consider required steps such as contact verification, promo-code entry, account linking, minimum activity, and other conditions.

Use conditions

We review where the bonus can be used, wagering, expiry, bet restrictions, and whether users can decline or cancel the bonus balance.

Withdrawal of received value

For skins, crypto, and internal balance, minimums, fees, item availability, and ordinary account-verification rules all matter.

Advertising transparency

The page should clearly distinguish the free reward from any deposit bonus rather than combining unrelated benefits into one maximum number without explanation.

Offer freshness

Promo codes and welcome campaigns change quickly, so CSGOBROKER updates the ranking when reward size, composition, or terms materially change.

Why Sign-Up Bonuses Change

Platforms regularly replace promo codes, free cases, internal-currency amounts, and new-user requirements. An old code can stop working while a familiar campaign receives a different reward bundle.

Welcome offers can also vary by traffic source, country, or partner campaign. The same platform may therefore display different introductory rewards to different users.

After registering, compare daily rewards, progressive rewards, rakeback, and other promotions in the main bonuses section.

Safety When Claiming a Sign-Up Bonus

A legitimate welcome reward does not require sending your password or secret security codes to a platform employee.

Verify the official domain

Phishing copies often use a free-bonus promise to direct users into a fake Steam or third-party login window.

Never share security codes

Receiving a bonus does not require sending anyone your password, Steam Guard code, or two-factor backup codes.

Be careful with required deposits

If a supposedly free welcome offer requires funding, treat it as a deposit promotion and do not deposit more than planned.

Read the rules before entering a promo code

Check whether the code replaces another welcome offer and whether the choice can be changed after activation.

Follow age and regional rules

When the reward is connected to gambling, platform rules and local legal requirements apply. Use responsible gambling resources.

Frequently Asked Questions About Sign-Up Bonuses

What is a sign-up bonus?

A welcome reward for a new account, such as a free case, balance, promo code, discount, free attempt, or another introductory benefit.

Is every sign-up bonus completely free?

No. Some campaigns require a first deposit or another paid action. The free and deposit components should be separated.

When should I enter the promo code?

It depends on the site: during registration, afterward, or before the first deposit. The correct stage should be stated in the promotion terms.

Can I receive several welcome bonuses?

A platform can bundle several rewards, but separate promo codes or affiliate offers usually cannot be combined unless the service explicitly allows it.

Can a free case or balance be withdrawn?

It depends on the rules. Minimum withdrawals, wagering, mode restrictions, or verification can apply.

Why was my bonus not credited?

Possible reasons include an invalid promo code, repeat registration, unsupported region, missed verification, or another unmet promotion condition.

How is a sign-up bonus different from a deposit bonus?

A sign-up bonus is connected to creating a new account, while a deposit bonus requires funding.

Why does the CSGOBROKER ranking change?

Promo codes, free rewards, registration requirements, withdrawal rules, regional availability, and active campaigns can all change.
